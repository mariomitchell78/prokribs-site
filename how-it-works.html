How It Works

Simple. Fast. Trusted.

ProKribs makes finding or listing athlete housing straightforward. Here is everything you need to know.

For Renters

✅
1. Get Verified
Create your profile and verify your athlete status. Keeps the community exclusive. Takes 5 minutes.
🔍
2. Browse and Request
Search verified properties in your city. Filter by dates, budget, bedrooms. Send a booking request to the host.
📋
3. Sign and Pay
Review and sign your lease digitally. Pay your first month securely through Stripe.
🏡
4. Move In
Get your keys and settle in. Monthly rent auto-pays on the same day each month.
⭐
5. Leave a Review
After your stay, leave a review. This keeps the community accountable and trusted for everyone.
📱
App Coming Soon
iOS and Android app launching soon.

For Hosts

🏠
1. List Your Home
Submit your property details, photos, and monthly rate. We review every listing within 24 hours.
✅
2. Get Approved
Our team verifies your property meets ProKribs standards and activates your listing.
🤝
3. Accept Bookings
Review renter profiles and accept or decline at your discretion. You stay in control.
💰
4. Get Paid
Monthly rent deposits directly to your bank. Automated, reliable, on time every month.

Payments and Fees

ProKribs charges a 5% platform fee deducted from the host monthly payout. There are no listing fees, no monthly subscriptions, and no hidden charges. We only make money when you do.

The 31+ Day Rule

All ProKribs stays are a minimum of 31 consecutive days. This classifies every booking as a long-term residential tenancy, keeping you exempt from short-term rental taxes and regulations in most cities including Nevada, California, Texas, and Illinois.
