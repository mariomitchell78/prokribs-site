Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026 · ProKribs LLC · Las Vegas, Nevada

1. Information We Collect

We collect name, email, phone, payment info (via Stripe), athlete verification documents, and usage data when you use ProKribs.

2. How We Use It

To verify your identity, process bookings and payments, send notifications, and improve the platform. We do not sell your data.

3. Information Sharing

We share necessary info between hosts and renters to complete bookings. We use Stripe, Firebase, Twilio, SendGrid, and AWS as service providers.

4. Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Payments are processed by Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant). We never store card numbers.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time. Contact privacy@prokribs.com.
