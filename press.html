About ProKribs

ProKribs is the exclusive athlete-to-athlete housing marketplace connecting professional athletes with verified luxury housing. The platform facilitates long-term furnished rentals of 31+ days across 50+ markets in the United States.

Key Facts

  • Headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada
  • Serves professional athletes across all major sports leagues
  • All stays are 31+ days — long-term residential leases
  • 100% verified member community
  • Secure payment processing via Stripe
  • iOS and Android app coming soon
