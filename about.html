Our Mission

ProKribs was built to solve a problem every professional athlete knows too well: getting traded, signed, or relocated on short notice with nowhere to go. Finding quality furnished housing in a new city — fast, privately, without the stress of dealing with strangers — shouldn't be this hard.

We created ProKribs to be the platform we wish existed. An exclusive athlete-to-athlete marketplace where professional athletes can list their homes to fellow pros, and where athletes in transition can find trusted move-in-ready housing in any major market.

For Renters

Whether you just got the call, signed a new deal, or need a base for training camp — ProKribs gets you into a quality furnished home fast. All stays are 31+ days, structured as long-term residential leases so you stay outside short-term rental regulations and pricing.

For Hosts

Your home is an asset. ProKribs helps you monetize it when you're on the road, playing overseas, or have a property sitting empty. List in under 10 minutes, set your price, and let the platform handle everything from vetting tenants to processing payments.

📍 ProKribs is headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada and operates in 50+ markets across the United States. Our mobile app for iOS and Android is coming soon.
